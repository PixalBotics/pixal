## Notes
- {{issue_1}} — pull the top issue from the audit (e.g. "the site still shows placeholder template text where your contact info should be").
- {{proof_point}} — e.g. "+140% organic traffic in 90 days for a similar local client" (from our case-study posts).
- Keep it short — 80-120 words. No attachments on first email (avoids spam filters). Follow-up email can include the audit summary.
- Always personalize the opening line with something specific from their actual site — generic mail-merge blasts get ignored/marked spam.
